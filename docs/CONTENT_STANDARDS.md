# CONTENT STANDARDS

## Dr. Linus Uche Emeanulu — Academic Portfolio

---

## 1. Source Quality

Acceptable sources for academic content, in order of preference:

1. **Official university pages** — verified institutional profiles, faculty pages, department listings.
2. **Publisher records** — journal websites, book publisher pages, DOI records.
3. **Verified academic profiles** — ORCID, Google Scholar, ResearchGate, Academia.edu (when confirmed to belong to the correct person).
4. **Original academic documents** — supplied by Dr. Linus directly (CV, certificates, transcripts, letters).
5. **Reputable academic databases** — Scopus, Web of Science, Crossref.

Unacceptable sources:

- Social media posts (unless verified as official).
- Unverified blog posts or articles.
- AI-generated content.
- Assumptions based on name matching alone.

---

## 2. Identity Verification

Before using any online source, confirm it refers to **the correct Dr. Linus Uche Emeanulu** and not a different person with a similar name.

Verification steps:

1. Cross-reference at least two independent sources that agree on institutional affiliation, research area, or publication record.
2. Check that the source's listed name, institution, and field are consistent with other verified information.
3. If identity cannot be confirmed, record the source in `RESEARCH_REGISTER.md` as **unverified** and do not use the information.

---

## 3. Publication Metadata Checks

For each publication, verify:

- **Title** — exact title as published.
- **Authors** — full author list in the correct order, with correct spelling.
- **Journal / Publisher** — full journal name or publisher name.
- **Year** — publication or acceptance year.
- **Volume / Issue / Pages** — if applicable.
- **DOI** — Digital Object Identifier, if available.
- **Type** — journal article, book, book chapter, conference paper, etc.

Record each publication's source URL in `RESEARCH_REGISTER.md`. Prefer linking to the original publication page or DOI rather than reproducing full text.

---

## 4. Copyright Considerations

- **Do not** copy copyrighted full-text publications into the website without permission or a valid basis for use.
- **Do not** fabricate certificates, CVs, publication covers, institutional logos, endorsements, or research documents.
- **Do** link to original publication pages and DOI records.
- **Do** use only approved photographs, CV files, and documents supplied by Dr. Linus or obtained from sources that permit the intended use.
- **Do** record the source and usage basis for any document or image in `RESEARCH_REGISTER.md`.

---

## 5. Verification Statuses

Every piece of academic content must carry one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Verified** | Confirmed against at least two independent reliable sources, or supplied directly by Dr. Linus. |
| **Unverified** | Found in a source but identity or accuracy not yet confirmed. Do not publish. |
| **Pending** | Awaiting verification or source gathering. Do not publish. |
| **Placeholder** | Development placeholder only. Clearly marked. Never presented as genuine. |
| **Approved** | Verified and explicitly approved by Dr. Linus for public release. |

Only content marked **Approved** may appear on the live website as professional information.

---

## 6. Professional Content Approval Process

1. **Gather**: Collect content from reliable sources or from Dr. Linus directly.
2. **Verify**: Confirm identity and accuracy using the steps above.
3. **Record**: Log the content, source, and verification status in `RESEARCH_REGISTER.md`.
4. **Draft**: Add content to the website with its verification status noted in the source code or data file.
5. **Review**: Present the draft content to Dr. Linus for review.
6. **Approve**: Dr. Linus confirms the content is accurate and may be published.
7. **Publish**: Mark the content as **Approved** and make it visible on the website.

No content representing Dr. Linus's professional identity is published without his explicit approval.

---

## 7. Placeholder Usage

During development, placeholders may be used where content is expected but not yet available.

Placeholders must:

- Be clearly labelled as placeholders in the code.
- Never appear to be genuine professional information.
- Not include fabricated names, dates, institutions, titles, or statistics.
- Be replaced with verified, approved content before the page is published.

Current placeholder pages use a shared `PlaceholderPage` component that displays a generic description and a link back to Home.
