## Publication Verification — Evidence Requirements

Before any publication record is added to the public-facing dataset (`src/data/publications.js` with `verified: true`), the following evidence must be confirmed:

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Complete title | The exact title as published, confirmed against the original source. |
| Author list and order | Full author list in the correct order, with correct spelling. |
| Identity confirmation | Confirmation that Dr. Linus Uche Emeanulu is the intended author, not a namesake. This requires cross-referencing at least two independent sources (e.g., institutional page and publisher record) that agree on name, affiliation, and research area. |
| Publication year | The publication or acceptance year, confirmed against the publisher record. |
| Publication venue | The full journal name, conference name, book title, or publisher name. |
| DOI or source URL | The DOI or a stable URL to the original publisher or institutional source, when available. |
| Publication type | Journal article, book, book chapter, conference paper, thesis, or report. |
| Abstract (optional) | A short abstract or description, only if verified against the original source. |
| Remaining uncertainty | Any unresolved questions about the record must be noted in the `verificationNotes` field and recorded here. |

If a record cannot be independently verified from reliable sources, it must be marked as requiring verification and excluded from the public-facing data.

### Verification Process

1. Locate the publication in a reliable source (publisher record, DOI database, institutional repository, or academic profile confirmed to belong to Dr. Linus).
2. Confirm identity by cross-referencing at least two independent sources.
3. Record the source URL and verification details in the Source Log below.
4. Add the publication to `src/data/publications.js` with `verified: true` and fill in the `source` and `verificationNotes` fields.
5. Present the record to Dr. Linus for approval.
6. Once approved, update the register status to Approved.
